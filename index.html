        Both Render APIs and exported Lua helpers are supported. Native menu UI,
        Panorama and cached function aliases can bypass the recorder. “All
        calls” includes entries that cannot be drawn in the preview.
      

      

        Text and effects are approximations. Clipping and alpha are applied when
        captured. Render-target content and unknown helpers remain inspectable
        as data. Copied Lua reconstructs values; it does not recover original
        variables or callback code.
      

      

        Shortcuts: Ctrl/Cmd-click adds layers, Ctrl/Cmd-A selects visible parts,
        Ctrl/Cmd-C copies Lua, Delete hides, Escape clears. The example is
        synthetic.
